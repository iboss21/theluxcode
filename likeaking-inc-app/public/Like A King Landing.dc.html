What industries do you work with? +

We partner with mid-market and enterprise businesses across finance, healthcare, retail, and professional services — any organisation that treats digital infrastructure as core to the business.

How does an engagement typically start? +

A discovery consult scopes your goals, current stack, and constraints. Within a week you'll have a fixed-scope proposal or a phased roadmap, whichever fits the work.

Do you handle security and compliance? +

Yes — every engagement includes security review by default. For regulated clients we scope formal penetration testing and compliance documentation aligned to NIST, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 as a dedicated workstream.

Can you integrate AI into an existing product? +

Yes. We audit your current systems, then design RAG pipelines, agents, or function-calling workflows that plug into what you already have — no rip-and-replace required.

What does ongoing support look like? +

Managed clients get 24/7 monitoring, proactive maintenance, and a direct line to the engineers who built the system — not a ticket queue.
